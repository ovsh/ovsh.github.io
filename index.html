Tools I built

  • Hypermark (2026) is for people making video with AI.

    An agent works with you on story, script, visuals, and editing, and keeps characters and voices consistent. It made both shows above.

  • Dailies (2026) is for production video editors.

    Built for a friend who's an Emmy-nominated video editor. Chat with thousands of hours of footage and pull the clips you need. Media never leaves the machine.

  • AI Native (2025) is for anyone who feels behind on AI.

    Learn by doing: hands-on lessons that play like a game and build real fluency fast.

  • Loadout (2025) is for people with lots of AI skills.

    See every skill your coding tools have, switch each one on or off from the Mac menu bar, and edit agent prompts in place. Built for myself.

  • PowerWatch (2025) is for anyone whose MacBook battery dies too fast.

    Shows real drain in watts, live, and catches the app that's hogging it.

  • Kids Drawing Magic (2023) was for my kids.

    Kids drew scribbles and watched them turn into beautiful drawings — an early exploration of Stable Diffusion and ControlNet, right when ControlNet came out. Went viral on Reddit. Retired.
