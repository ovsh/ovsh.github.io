Tools I built

  • Hypermark is for people making video with AI.

    An agent works with you on story, script, visuals, and editing, and keeps characters and voices consistent. I made both shows above with it.

  • PowerWatch is for anyone with a MacBook.

    Shows real battery drain in watts, live, and names the responsible app.

  • Dailies is for production video editors.

    Built for one in particular: a friend, an Emmy-nominated editor. Chat with thousands of hours of footage and pull the clips you need. Media never leaves the machine.

  • Loadout is for people who run a lot of AI skills.

    See every skill your coding tools have, switch each one on or off from the Mac menu bar, and edit agent prompts in place. Built for myself.
