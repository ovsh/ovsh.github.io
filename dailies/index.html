Script view ground rules
BriefTextFrameScript

Your media stays put. Here is exactly what moves.

Stays on your computerThe footage, audio, proxies, and index database. Files are read in place; the index lives in your operating system's app-data folder and can be deleted and rebuilt at any time.
Sent to OpenRouterTranscript excerpts and embedding inputs, plus your questions. One API key powers everything. Electron protects it with Keychain on macOS or DPAPI on Windows when secure storage is available.
Honest answersEvery clip card is backed by the index. When nothing matches, the agent says it found nothing instead of inventing a hit.
Honest failuresA corrupt file becomes a visible error card with a retry button. Timecode that had to fall back to 30 fps is labeled as such in every export.
